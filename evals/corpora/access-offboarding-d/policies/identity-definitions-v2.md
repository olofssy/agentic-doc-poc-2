# Identity and Access Definitions, version 2

## IDD-2.1 — Workforce identity

A workforce identity is an identity assigned to an employee or contractor who is
authorised to use a corporate system.

## IDD-2.5 — Privileged access

Privileged access is access that can administer a corporate system or alter its
security configuration.

## IDD-2.6 — Personnel on inbound assignment

Personnel assigned to this organisation by an external partner, and who work
under this organisation's direct supervision and control, hold a workforce
identity. They remain personnel of the external partner and are neither an
employee nor a contractor of this organisation.
