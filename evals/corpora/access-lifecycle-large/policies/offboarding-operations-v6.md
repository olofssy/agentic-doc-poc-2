# Offboarding Operations Procedure, version 6

## OOP-7.1 — Employee request

HR must submit an ordinary employee workspace-disablement request at the
recorded employment end time.

## OOP-7.2 — Queue handling

The identity operations team must process a valid employee request within one
business day.

## OOP-7.3 — Agency notification

An agency sponsor must notify identity operations when an assignment closes.

## OOP-7.4 — Evidence retention

Offboarding tickets must be retained for seven years.
