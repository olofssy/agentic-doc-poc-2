# Privileged Access Standard, version 5

## PAS-4.1 — Administrative separation

Administrative workspaces must be disabled immediately when a verified
separation event is recorded.

## PAS-4.2 — Break-glass accounts

Break-glass accounts are governed by the emergency identity procedure.

## PAS-4.3 — Temporary elevation

Temporary elevated entitlements expire at the approved end time.

## PAS-4.4 — Review evidence

Privileged-access reviews must retain approval evidence for two years.
