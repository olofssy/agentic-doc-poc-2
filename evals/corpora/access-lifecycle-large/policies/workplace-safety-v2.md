# Workplace Safety Policy, version 2

## WSP-1.1 — Safety induction

Workers must complete safety induction before entering a work site.

## WSP-1.2 — Contractor induction

Contractors must complete a local safety briefing before site work begins.

## WSP-1.3 — Site departure

Site managers must collect safety equipment when site work ends.

## WSP-1.4 — Information-system scope

This policy does not govern credentials for corporate information systems.
