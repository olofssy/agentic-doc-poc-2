# Nordic Identity Addendum, version 2

## NIA-2.1 — Contact language

Identity support requests for Nordic employees may be submitted in a local
language.

## NIA-2.2 — Directory names

Directory names must use approved local characters where supported.

## NIA-2.3 — Agency population

This addendum does not alter agency engagement completion obligations.

## NIA-2.4 — Shared support hours

The Nordic support queue operates during regional business hours.
