# Physical Credentials Policy, version 4

## PCP-2.1 — Building badge

Building badges may remain active until the end of the final working day to
allow equipment return.

## PCP-2.2 — Restricted floors

Restricted-floor badges must be removed when facilities receives termination
notice.

## PCP-2.3 — Visitor passes

Visitor passes expire at the end of the visit date.

## PCP-2.4 — Physical scope

This policy governs physical premises credentials, not corporate systems.
