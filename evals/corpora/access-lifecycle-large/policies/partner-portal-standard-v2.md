# Partner Portal Standard, version 2

## PPS-3.1 — Partner portal accounts

Partner portal accounts are limited to the partner portal and do not grant a
corporate workspace credential.

## PPS-3.2 — Renewal

Partner portal accounts expire after annual sponsor renewal.

## PPS-3.3 — Partner administrator

Partner administrators may manage partner portal invitations only.

## PPS-3.4 — Departure notice

Partners must report a departed portal user within five business days.
