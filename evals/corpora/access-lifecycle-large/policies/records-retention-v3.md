# Records Retention Policy, version 3

## RRP-3.1 — Offboarding records

Offboarding records must be retained for seven years after separation.

## RRP-3.2 — Access approvals

Access approval records must be retained for two years after expiry.

## RRP-3.3 — Privacy requests

Privacy deletion requests must be handled through the records office.

## RRP-3.4 — Retention scope

Record retention does not determine account disablement deadlines.
