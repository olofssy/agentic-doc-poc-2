# Emergency Identity Procedure, version 2

## EID-2.1 — Break-glass account

A break-glass account may remain available during an active declared emergency.

## EID-2.2 — Emergency owner

The incident commander must name an emergency account owner.

## EID-2.3 — Emergency closure

Emergency account access must be reviewed when the emergency closes.

## EID-2.4 — Separation scope

This procedure applies only to designated break-glass accounts.
