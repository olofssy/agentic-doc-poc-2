# Business Continuity Policy, version 3

## BCP-5.1 — Continuity staffing

Business continuity staffing plans must identify accountable emergency roles.

## BCP-5.2 — Essential systems

Essential systems must have an approved recovery plan.

## BCP-5.3 — Temporary access

Temporary continuity access must have an expiry time.

## BCP-5.4 — Policy relationship

Continuity planning does not relax identity separation requirements.
