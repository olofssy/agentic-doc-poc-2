# Training Access Procedure, version 2

## TAP-3.1 — Training tenant

Training tenant accounts may remain active until the training session closes.

## TAP-3.2 — Course completion

Course access expires thirty days after course completion.

## TAP-3.3 — Instructor approval

Instructors must approve external learner access.

## TAP-3.4 — Production scope

Training tenant accounts do not grant production corporate-system access.
