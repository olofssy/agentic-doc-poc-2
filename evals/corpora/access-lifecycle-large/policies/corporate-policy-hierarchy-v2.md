# Corporate Policy Hierarchy, version 2

## CPH-1.1 — Corporate policy

A current corporate policy governs unless a more specific current local addendum
explicitly changes the same obligation.

## CPH-1.2 — Standards

A corporate standard may add controls but may not relax a corporate policy.

## CPH-1.3 — Procedures

A procedure describes operating steps and may not create a policy exception.

## CPH-1.4 — Supersession

A document marked superseded is not an active source of obligations.
