# Procurement Supplier Policy, version 4

## PSP-7.1 — Supplier engagement

Supplier engagements must have a signed statement of work.

## PSP-7.2 — Supplier end date

The procurement record must include a supplier engagement end date.

## PSP-7.3 — Contract handover

Procurement may retain supplier contact details for contract handover.

## PSP-7.4 — Access scope

This policy does not authorise supplier access to corporate systems.
