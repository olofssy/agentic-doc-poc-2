# Enterprise Identity Policy, version 7

## EIP-3.1 — Separation deactivation

An employment-affiliated security principal must be deactivated no later than
24 hours after a verified separation event.

## EIP-3.2 — Elevated entitlements

Elevated entitlements assigned to an employment-affiliated security principal
must be removed immediately when a verified separation event is recorded.

## EIP-3.3 — Shared credentials

Shared credentials must be transferred to an accountable owner before the
separating principal is deactivated.

## EIP-3.4 — Service principals

This policy does not govern non-human service principals.
