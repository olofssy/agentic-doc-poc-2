# Vendor Service Management Procedure, version 3

## VSM-8.1 — Support operator request

A supplier support operator must have a named internal service owner.

## VSM-8.2 — Ticket closure

The service owner must close supplier support tickets after an engagement ends.

## VSM-8.3 — Session review

Supplier support sessions must be reviewed after a major incident.

## VSM-8.4 — Contract evidence

The vendor management record must retain the engagement statement of work.
