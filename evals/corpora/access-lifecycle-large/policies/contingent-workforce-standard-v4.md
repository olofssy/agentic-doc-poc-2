# Contingent Workforce Standard, version 4

## CWS-5.1 — Engagement sponsor

Every agency engagement must have a sponsoring manager accountable for its
workspace credential and business-system access.

## CWS-5.2 — Handover continuity

At engagement completion, a sponsoring manager may keep an agency workspace
credential active for up to five calendar days to complete documented handover.

## CWS-5.3 — Assignment closure

The sponsor must record an assignment closure date in the workforce register.

## CWS-5.4 — Restricted workspaces

Agency workers may not be assigned elevated administrative workspaces unless a
separate privileged-access approval has been recorded.
